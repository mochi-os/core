## Yini esiyiqoqayo

Lapho ubhalisa futhi usebenzise le seva siqoqa:

- **Idatha ye-akhawunti**: igama lomsebenzisi, izindlela zokuqinisekisa ozikhethile (ikheli le-imeyili, ama-passkey, i-app yokuqinisekisa, amakhodi okutakula, noma i-akhawunti evela kumhlinzeki wokungena weqembu lesithathu), nanoma yiluphi ulwazi lwephrofayili olungezayo (igama elibonisiwe, isithombe somuntu, ibhayografi).
- **Okuqukethwe okudalayo**: ukuthumela, imiyalezo, amazwana, amafayela, nanoma yiyiphi enye idatha okhetha ukuyishicilela ngezinhlelo zokusebenza ezifakwe kule seva.
- **Okuqukethwe okuvela kwabanye abantu**: okuthunyelwe, imiyalezo, nemininingwane yephrofayili yabantu abakwamanye amaseva e-Mochi nakwamanye amanethiwekhi, okufika kule seva ngoba wena noma abanye abasebenzisi niyabalandela, nibhalisile kubo, noma nixhumana nabo ngemiyalezo.
- **Idatha yobuchwepheshe**: ikheli le-IP, ulwazi lwesiphequluli/idivayisi, nezitembu zezikhathi zezicelo ozenzayo. Lokhu kusetshenziselwa ukugcina insiza iphephile futhi isebenza, futhi ukuze kutholakale ukuxhashazwa.
- **Amakhukhi**: ikhukhi lesikhathi sokungena lisethwa lapho ungena. Ligcina kuphela isikhombi sesikhathi sokungena esingaqondiwe; asisebenzisi amakhukhi ukukhangisa noma ukulandelela kumasayithi ahlukene.

Asidayisi idatha yakho yomuntu siqu, futhi asabelane ngayo nabakhangisi.

## Indlela i-Mochi ephatha ngayo idatha yakho (lokhu kubalulekile)

I-Mochi yi-**inethiwekhi yontanga (P2P)**, hhayi isikhungo seseva eyodwa. Lokho kushintsha ukuthi idatha yakho ihamba kanjani ngezindlela ezibalulekile zobumfihlo bakho:

- Okuqukethwe okushicilela kubalandeli, ababhalisi, noma ezindaweni ezabiwayo **kuyakhushulwa kwamanye amaseva e-Mochi** asingethe labo balandeli noma ababhalisi. {{operator.name}} ngeke akwazi ukususa amakhophi okuqukethwe kwakho kumaseva angawasebenzisi. Uma ususa okuthunyelwe, le seva izothumela isaziso sokususa kontanga eyithumele kubo ngaphambili, kodwa ukususa kungumzamo omuhle futhi kuxhomeke ekubambisaneni kontanga laba.
- **Ubunikazi bakho (igama lomsebenzisi, iphrofayili, amakhi omphakathi)** kushicilelwa kuhla lwemibhalo lwe-Mochi uma ukhetha iphrofayili eyomphakathi, ukuze abanye abasebenzisi be-Mochi bakwazi ukukuthola futhi baxhumane nawe.
- Okuqukethwe okuqondile, okuyimfihlo (ama-draft, amanothi ayimfihlo, imiyalezo eyimfihlo phakathi kwabamukeli abathile) kuhlala kule seva nakumaseva abamukeli kuphela; akusiyo ingxenye yenethiwekhi ka-P2P yomphakathi.
- Lapho le seva ikuvumela **ukuxhuma kwamanye amanethiwekhi** - ngokuxhumanisa i-akhawunti onayo kwenye insiza, noma ngokusingatha lapha i-akhawunti abantu abakwamanye amanethiwekhi abangayilandela noma bayithumelele imiyalezo - lokho okushicilelayo noma okuthumelayo ngaleyo akhawunti kuya kumaseva akulawo manethiwekhi. Awawona amaseva e-Mochi futhi {{operator.name}} akawasebenzisi. I-akhawunti enjalo ingeyomphakathi kunethiwekhi yayo, futhi kwamanye amanethiwekhi ama-akhawunti owalandelayo nokuthunyelwe okuthandile nakho kungokomphakathi. Ukuhlela noma ukususa okuthile kamuva kuthumela isicelo esingase singanakwa yilawo maseva.

Mfushane: noma yini okhetha ukuyabelana nabanye abantu, ku-Mochi noma kwamanye amanethiwekhi, izohamba ngaphandle kokulawula kuka-{{operator.name}}, ngokwakha. Phatha okuqukethwe okushicilela njengoba ungenza nge-imeyili noma kunoma yikuphi ukuxhumana okuhlanganisiwe.

## Amaqembu esithathu

Sabelana ngedatha eyincane yomuntu siqu nemiqondisi yamaqembu esithathu alandelayo lapho usebenzisa izici ezihambisanayo:

- **Insiza ye-imeyili**: ama-imeyili aphumayo (amakhodi okungena, ukuqinisekiswa kwekheli, izaziso) athunyelwa ngomhlinzeki we-SMTP ohlanganiswa ngu-{{operator.name}}.
- **Abahlinzeki bokungena beqembu lesithathu**: uma ungena nge-Google, GitHub, Microsoft, Facebook, noma omunye umhlinzeki we-OAuth, lowo mhlinzeki uyezwa ukuthi ungene kule seva futhi wabelane ngekheli lakho le-imeyili nedatha eyisisekelo yephrofayili nathi.
- **Abahlinzeki bezinkokhelo**: uma uthenga ngohlelo lokusebenza lwemakethe, inkokhelo icutshungulwa nge-Stripe (noma yimuphi umhlinzeki wezinkokhelo o-{{operator.name}} ahlanganisile). Asigcini imininingwane ephelele yamakhadi.
- **Amanye amanethiwekhi**: uma uxhumanisa i-akhawunti onayo kwenye insiza, leyo nsiza ithola lokho okuthumelayo ngayo, futhi le seva igcina imininingwane yokungena eyivumela ukuthi isebenze egameni lakho lapho. Uma usingatha lapha i-akhawunti yenye inethiwekhi, amaseva nezinsiza zaleyo nethiwekhi zithola lokho okushicilelayo kuyo, kanye nekheli lomphakathi le-akhawunti.
